NeoReader — Welcome

NeoReader is an offline ebook reader with Material Design 3 and frosted glass UI.

Supported in this build:
• EPUB, PDF, TXT
• MOBI / AZW / AZW3 (DRM-free text extract)
• FictionBook FB2
• Comic CBZ (and ZIP-based CBR)
• Markdown, HTML / XHTML / MHTML
• DOCX text extract

Open Import to add your own books. Everything stays on device — no account, no cloud.
