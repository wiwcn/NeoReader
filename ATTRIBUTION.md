inspired by [Koodo Reader](https://github.com/koodo-reader/koodo-reader)
(AGPL-3.0) and the personal offline fork documented in
`koodo-reader-offline`.

NeoReader does **not** copy Koodo Reader’s Electron/React source. It reimplements
a mobile-primary offline reader in Flutter under **AGPL-3.0**, with Material
