# Attribution

**NeoReader** is original Flutter application code. Its *feature scope* is
inspired by the open-source desktop/web project
[Koodo Reader](https://github.com/koodo-reader/koodo-reader) (AGPL-3.0) and
community offline forks — as a product blueprint for an offline ebook reader —
**not** as a port or copy of Koodo’s source tree.

NeoReader reimplements a mobile-primary offline reader in Flutter under
**GPL-3.0**, with Material Design 3 + frosted-glass UI, unlimited local library,
and no cloud / account / Pro gates.
